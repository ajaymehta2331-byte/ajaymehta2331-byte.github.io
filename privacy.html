Privacy Policy – Advait Uploader

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Advait Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the "अद्वैत (Advait)" YouTube channel. It uploads the owner's own daily videos, subtitles and a first comment to the owner's own YouTube channel, and adds the videos to the channel's playlists.

What data is used

The tool uses Google sign-in (YouTube Data API) only for the channel owner's own account. It does not ask any other person to sign in, and it does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of viewers or other users.

Google user data

Access to the owner's YouTube account is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos. The access token is stored privately as a secret and is not shared with anyone. Advait Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
